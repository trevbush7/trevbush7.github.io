Affiliations

American Society of Pharmacognosy (ASP)

Member

Established in 1959, ASP is an international, nonprofit scientific society whose members are committed to promoting and developing the science of pharmacognosy — the study of natural product molecules useful for their medicinal, ecological, or other functional properties — and all aspects of the natural products sciences.

Consortium for the Globalization of Chinese Medicine (CGCM)

Not a member · Presented 2026

Founded in 2003, CGCM is a global, non-profit organization advancing the field of Chinese herbal medicine to benefit humankind through collaboration among academics, industry, and regulatory agencies worldwide — including its goal of bringing Chinese herbal medicine into the cornerstone of future medicine where "West meets East," the same vision behind my PI's concept of WE Medicine. My PI, Professor Yung-Chi Cheng, serves as Chairman.

American Council for Medicinally Active Plants (ACMAP)

Not a member · Presented 2026

Founded in 2007, ACMAP is a nonprofit organized for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes. It promotes and fosters research, development, production, and conservation of medicinal, aromatic, and other bioactive plants useful to human health, and hosts an annual conference alongside its peer-reviewed Journal of Medicinally Active Plants (JMAP).

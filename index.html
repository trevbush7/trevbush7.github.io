About Me

I am a Pharmacology PhD student at Yale working at the intersection of mass spectrometry–based lipidomics, proteomics, and network pharmacology.

My research operates in two complementary directions. First, I use a multi-omics systems biology approach to map how lipid and protein networks become dysregulated in aging and chronic inflammatory disease, elucidating enzymatic and signaling rewiring across tissues and cell types. Second, I apply network pharmacology to investigate the polypharmacological and synergistic mechanisms by which natural products modulate multiple molecular targets simultaneously to restore cellular resilience and promote regeneration.
